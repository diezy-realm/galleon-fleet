# Marketing Content Delivery SOP

## Steps

1. **Draft Campaign Copy and Visual Assets** — Draft promotional copy, newsletter text, and creative asset briefs according to brand voice.
   - do: Adhere to brand voice guidelines and target audience tone
   - do: Include required call-to-action (CTA) links and disclosures
   - dont: Do not make unsubstantiated product claims
   - dont: Do not use copyrighted images without clearance

2. **Editorial and Brand Review** — Review draft content for brand consistency, factual accuracy, and regulatory compliance.
   - do: Cross-check all claims against product truth sources
   - do: Verify tone of voice and grammatical precision
   - dont: Do not approve drafts with unverified claims

3. **Distribution and Multi-Channel Broadcast** — Schedule and publish the approved content across selected distribution channels.
   - do: Schedule broadcast during peak engagement windows
   - do: Monitor early engagement metrics and feedback
   - dont: Do not post unapproved variations
